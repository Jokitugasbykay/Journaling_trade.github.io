Daily Loss Limit (Daily Max Loss)

Maximum 5% of initial balance per day. For a $1,000 account, the maximum daily cumulative loss is $50.00.

Maximum Account Drawdown (Max Trailing DD)

Maximum 10% of peak balance. Limit risk per trade at 0.5% - 1.0% to ensure you have at least 10-20 opportunities.

Institutional Execution Rules

Never enlarge lots after experiencing a loss (Revenge trading). Always place Stop Loss at the same time when opening an order.
